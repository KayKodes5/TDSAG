Down syndrome is a chromosomal condition that is present from conception. It is not a disease, it is not contagious, and it cannot be cured. It can cause delays in intellectual, physical and cognitive development.

In South Africa it is the largest single cause of intellectual disability, affecting about 1 in every 500 children born each year. Many people with Down syndrome attend mainstream schools, work as actors, models, musicians, athletes, IT specialists and motivational speakers, and live long, fulfilling lives into their fifties and beyond.
