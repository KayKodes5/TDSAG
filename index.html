Who are we? Our social mandate

TDSAG is a registered non-profit organisation, established in 1986.

We work to improve the quality of life of all people born with Down syndrome and other intellectual disabilities, to promote their right to live with independence and dignity, and to be active members of their communities.

We respect religious and cultural diversity, and prioritise our work in disadvantaged communities.

What do we do?

We provide free Outreach Support Groups, Early Intervention and Self-Advocacy Training Programmes to people and their families living with Down syndrome and other intellectual disabilities, from birth throughout adulthood.

Why is early intervention so important?

In South Africa, Down syndrome is still culturally stigmatised. Some parents are led to believe their child’s disability is their fault, and children miss out on education and health care. Early support changes that.
