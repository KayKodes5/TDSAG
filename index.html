Who we are

Awareness around abilities, not disabilities.

TDSAG is a registered non-profit organisation, established in 1986. We work to improve the quality of life of all people born with Down syndrome and other intellectual disabilities, and to promote their right to live with independence and dignity.

Children born with Down syndrome can and do grow up to live long, happy and fulfilled lives. Given the right opportunities and support, they are active members of society.

Current campaign

Keep the Learning Centre Alive

A place to learn. A future to build.

Our learning centre helped young people and adults with Down syndrome build independence, life and job skills, and routes into work and community life. It stopped operating because we could no longer meet the running costs.

This campaign builds a steady funding base so we can restore and sustain these learning opportunities. We will share regular updates on what has been raised, what it has funded and what is still needed.

Your gift helps sustain

  • Self-advocacy and rights training
  • Life, literacy and job-readiness skills
  • Individual development plans with families
  • Learnerships and supported employment
  • Skills and work creation for unemployed mothers and young adults
