For children and their families

Outreach support & early intervention

Children born with Down syndrome are the same as any other child, only differently-abled. Our outreach programme gives them, and the families who care for them, ongoing support from the very start.

Who it’s for

  • Children with Down syndrome and other intellectual disabilities
  • Parents, families and caregivers
  • Communities affected by poverty and social exclusion

What’s involved

  • Childhood development and stimulation
  • Health checks and therapy
  • Families programme: individual and group counselling
  • Self-help (support) group development
  • Home-based care and skills training
  • Awareness and education

We also help children, youth and adults who have suffered abuse or neglect to reach professional counselling and safe alternative accommodation.

For youth and adults

Self-advocacy, life & job skills

We empower youth and adults with Down syndrome and other intellectual and physical disabilities to speak up for themselves, have their rights upheld, and be respected as individuals.

Who it’s for

  • Youth and adults with Down syndrome and other intellectual and physical disabilities
  • Learners in their last phase of school at Schools of Specialised Education

What’s involved

  • Identifying each person’s capabilities, with targeted support
  • Courses in independence, literacy and job skills
  • Specialised learnerships and supported employment
  • Individual development plans, with families included
George Lesotho completed several learnerships through the programme and went on to full-time work on Eskom-based projects. He chaired the TDSAG Self-Advocacy Group and captained the Special Olympics national hockey team in Japan.

For mothers, youth and adults

Community upliftment

Our skills training and work creation programme runs training alongside real work opportunities. Our job is to find the skills in people, not to force them into roles that don’t suit them.

Who it’s for

  • Unemployed mothers and youth
  • Adults with Down syndrome and other intellectual disabilities

What’s involved

  • Bespoke training that builds independence and confidence
  • Job skills training and work opportunities
  • Community, corporate and employee projects that create jobs
